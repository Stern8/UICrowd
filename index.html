How it works

Signing in turns the page into a stadium crowd display. The correct password is filled in, so press Enter. Change it to anything else to hear the other end.

  • Cards: every fan holds one board showing their slice of the page.
  • Tifo: one printed banner carried by the hands underneath it.
  • Shirts: each fan wears one pixel of the page. Needs a big crowd.

Shortcuts

  • 1 2 3 switch display, W Mexican wave, B boards or banner up/down, M mill about
  • F flat, P perspective, G pitchside, C close-up on the hands, R replay, S settings
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, shift-drag or right-drag to pan.
